# Growth Ideas

Post-launch feature ideas for driving adoption and retention, primarily targeting colleges and running clubs.

## Acquisition

- **Post-session share card** — "I made it home safe" shareable to Instagram stories / iMessage after completing a session. Free impressions from every use, normalizes the behavior socially.
- **Referral stats in-app** — Show ambassadors how many people signed up with their code. Turns campus reps into a competitive channel without building a formal program.
- **Group invite links** — A club captain shares one link, everyone joins each other's circles mutually in one step instead of N individual invitations.

## Activation

- **Deep link to first session** — Ambassador texts a link that opens straight to session creation, pre-populated with destination and duration. Reduces steps from download to first session.

## Retention

- **Home screen widget** — One-tap session start from the lock screen. Kills the friction of opening the app and configuring for repeat use cases like walking home from the bar.
- **Smart nudges** — "It's 11pm Friday — heading out?" based on past session patterns. Turns a tool they have to remember into one that remembers for them.
- **Session templates** — Recurring "Tuesday 6pm group run" template with pre-filled destination, duration, and circle. One-tap start for habitual use cases.

## Segment-specific

- **Apple Watch companion** — Runners don't always carry phones. Watch app that starts a session and sends location is a differentiator for the running club segment.
