🎨 Primary Color Alternatives

Moving away from red to convey safety, not danger

⚠️ Current Issue with Coral Red (#FF6B6B)

Red and red-adjacent colors trigger danger/alert responses in the brain. For a safety app where we want users to feel protected and calm, not anxious, we should avoid red as the primary color. Red should be reserved only for actual emergencies.

🎯 Recommendation: Calm Teal (#14B8A6)

Why Teal works best for Made It Home:

  • • Psychologically calming - reduces anxiety rather than triggering it
  • • Fresh and modern - appeals to college-age users
  • • Gender neutral - works for all users
  • • Distinct from emergency yellow - clear visual hierarchy
  • • Associated with health/healing - medical/safety connotations
  • • Works with existing secondary color - teal family consistency
