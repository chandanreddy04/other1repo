Design Analysis

✅ Fully Rounded (Pills)

  • • More playful and friendly
  • • Softer, less intimidating
  • • Better for single-line text
  • • Trendy, modern feel
  • • Great for tags/badges

✅ Rounded Squares

  • • More professional look
  • • Better text alignment
  • • Easier to stack/grid
  • • More space efficient
  • • Better for icons

🎯 Recommendation

For a college safety app: The fully rounded (pill) design feels more approachable and less institutional, which is important for reducing anxiety in emergency situations. However, rounded squares provide better information hierarchy and professional trust.

Hybrid approach: Use pills for primary actions and status badges (friendly), but rounded squares for cards and input fields (clarity).
